What gets synced

Once an order's first payment is logged, and the customer has a wedding date, it builds a schedule of up to five appointments. Pressing Sync on that order writes them to your primary Google Calendar as all-day events, with reminders 7 days and 3 days ahead. Syncing again moves the same events rather than adding new ones.

Follow-up reminders sync on their own as soon as you move a customer through the pipeline — one all-day event, reminding you the day before.

A wedding date recorded as a month only is treated as an estimate, and its schedule is not sent to Google until you confirm the exact day.
